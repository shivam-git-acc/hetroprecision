Qwen's outlier channels are what made per-token quantization so fragile. Quantizing keys per channel, as KIVI does, cuts key error three times, removes layer 0's special status and makes plain uniform 4-bit near-lossless. At 2-bit KIVI the model stays fluent but makes small factual and reasoning errors, which perplexity alone would hide. With a good quantizer, which heads you protect matters much less.
