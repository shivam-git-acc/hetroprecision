Plan of action · weeks 6–9

A fused, paged decode kernel with per-head bit-widths

What the data says the kernel must do

  1. Paged, pre-allocated cache, native grouped-query attention: no copy, no repeat_kv
  2. Dequantize inside attention, never the whole cache
  3. Keys per channel with a BF16 residual window
  4. Per-head bits: a few heads at 8–16, the rest at 2–4
  5. Measure dispatch cost: one grouped launch, byte-balanced split-K, occupancy and tail idle
