Measuring the Systems Cost of Heterogeneous KV Quantization

Shivam Patel (2025202030) · Suyash Pande (2025201063) · Divyanshu Jain (2025201059)

Our project asks whether giving different KV-cache heads different bit-widths is really free at inference time. Evaluation 1 covers the baseline, where the time goes, which heads matter, and what quantization costs when it is implemented naively versus in a fused kernel.
